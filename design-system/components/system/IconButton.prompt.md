Icon-only controls: the black × that closes a detail, grey chevrons, the composer's add-on squares.

```jsx
<IconButton variant="dark" label="Close"><Icon name="x" size={12} width={2.6} /></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="fill" size="lg" label="Collapse"><Icon name="down" size={14} width={2.2} /></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="outline" label="Attach a file"><Icon name="file" size={14} /></IconButton>
```
