# HYPE Innovation (Bonn): competitor profile for NextUp

Research date: 2 Nov 2026. Budget-limited pass (about 18 search/fetch calls). G2 and Capterra review pages returned HTTP 403, so the review evidence comes from search snippets and HYPE's own G2 announcement. Almost everything below is a **vendor claim** from hypeinnovation.com unless marked otherwise. This file extends `../NextUp psychographic segmentation/buyer_side_competitors.md`, and it corrects that file's automotive logo list (see Q7).

## Q0. What changed 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
HYPE became a roll-up backed by Main Capital (HLP, Viima, INNOSPOT, then Planbox in Feb 2024; about €30M revenue, 160+ staff). In Sep 2026 it relaunched as an "AI-first data platform" for multinationals and the public sector. The relaunch adds MCP/Copilot integration, a broad generative-AI suite and a "predictive" impact pitch. Viima now sells as HYPE Boards, the low-end SKU.

### Cited Findings
- Majority stakes in Viima (Finland; founded 2013, more than 10k organisations) and INNOSPOT (startup scouting) were taken in 2021 with Main Capital — [Borenius, 19 Mar 2021](https://borenius.com/references/borenius-advised-hype-innovation-on-its-acquisition-of-viima-solutions); [Deloitte Legal](https://www2.deloitte.com/dl/en/pages/legal/articles/transaction-advice-hype-mcp-viima-innospot.html); [Viima](https://www.viima.com/news/viima-joins-forces-with-hype)
- The HYPE + Planbox merger was announced 27 Feb 2024 (Bonn/Wilmington). It was Main Capital's fourth add-on since 2019, and the combined revenue approaches €30M. HYPE product lines are now HYPE Innovate, HYPE Partnering and HYPE Boards, with Planbox features folded in. These details come from search snippets, not full pages — [HYPE press release](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-acquires-planbox-bolstering-leadership-in-strategic-innovation-management); [Main Capital](https://main.nl/?p=9896); [HYPE FAQ](https://hypeinnovation.com/planbox-is-now-part-of-hype-innovation). Privsource gives a closing date of 25 Apr 2024, which conflicts with the announcement date — [privsource](https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/oMS3lZ)
- Viima was rebranded "HYPE Boards" together with the HYPE 11 release (per a LinkedIn post; year not verified) — [search result, LinkedIn](https://fi.linkedin.com/in/dianaporumboiu)
- **Strategic relaunch, 8 Sep 2026 (25th anniversary):** HYPE describes itself as "evolving from an enterprise innovation platform into an AI-first data platform", with a new CI, website and vision ("Turning innovation into value"). It names three shifts: predictive intelligence, automated lifecycle workflows and ecosystem integration via "MCP … existing AI agents, Copilot, Teams, and BI tools". CEO Sven Depner claims it will "project the business outcomes of innovations with remarkable precision" and move customers "beyond simple ROI estimations to measure true impact". Target customers are "multinational organizations and global public sector institutions". HQ is in Germany, with subsidiaries in the US, CA, FI and UK, and more than 160 employees — [HYPE relaunch PR](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-announces-strategic-relaunch)
- 2025 partnerships: FIBRES (AI foresight), and Amplify and rInnovation Group (ISO 56001-aligned services). HYPE appears in the Gartner Market Guide for Innovation Management Platforms for the 8th consecutive year (2025) — [HYPE news via search](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/innovation-management-in-2025-ai-is-taking-over-but-thats-not-enough)
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 renewed "for the sixth consecutive year" (2025). The first certification was in April 2019 — [HYPE ISO 2025](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-renews-iso-27001-certification-for-2025); [HYPE ISO 2019](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-receives-iso27001-certification)
- G2 Spring 2025: Leader in Idea Management (32 vendors), 4.8/5, 95% recommend. Satisfaction scores: Support 97%, Ease of Use 94%, Ease of Admin 92%, Ease of Setup 89% (category average 88%). This is vendor-published — [HYPE G2 PR, 6 Apr 2025](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/best-idea-management-software-of-spring-2025-hype-innovation-named-g2-leader)

### Inferences
- HYPE is a PE-backed consolidator. The 2026 relaunch moves it toward innovation portfolio/strategy and AI data, up-market of the shop-floor BVW. The "predict business outcomes" claim is the opposite of NextUp's "scores never from model output" position, which gives NextUp a clean contrast for works councils.
- The product surface is the result of several acquisitions (HYPE Enterprise, Planbox, Viima, INNOSPOT, HLP). Integration seams and a configuration-heavy setup are plausible consequences (see Q8 reviews).

### Gaps
- No public changelog or release notes for 2025–26 were found (searches for "2026 release" returned only Gartner Hype Cycle noise). The exact HYPE Boards rebrand date is unverified.

## Q1. Idea capture & effort for employees

### Takeaway
Capture is campaign- and form-based, with an AI Coach and translation (20+ languages). HYPE's pages give no evidence of QR codes, kiosk or shop-floor capture, or anonymous submission. Teams is used for embedding and notifications, not as a capture channel per se.

### Cited Findings
- The AI Coach gives contributors "real-time guidance on ideas and campaigns". The AI Idea Generator creates seed ideas from business goals. The Web Clipper browser extension adds web pages — [HYPE AI](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/ai-value-acceleration)
- AI-powered translation and support for 20+ languages — [HYPE idea management](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/idea-management-discovery)
- The BVW page describes one system "across teams, sites, departments and locations". It does not mention mobile, QR, offline or anonymity — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- Teams: "Embed initiative boards, share updates, and notify users directly in Teams channels". Also SharePoint/Viva Engage and Outlook — [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations)
- Translation engines: DeepL, Google, Azure — [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations)

### Inferences
- The employee effort is a classic web form with AI writing help. NextUp's guided chat ("ask first, then raise") and low-effort frontline capture are not visibly matched.

### Gaps
- Mobile app, QR/kiosk and anonymous submission were not confirmed or refuted on HYPE pages. Ask in a demo.

## Q2. AI assistance, explainability and human-in-the-loop

### Takeaway
The suite is broad: Coach, Idea Generator, Evaluator Summary, Similarity Search, Triage (completeness and duplicates), Campaign Assistant (natural-language queries), Trend Generator, Innovation Graph, Startup Scouting, AI reviewer suggestion, AI savings estimation, an MCP server and customer-specific LLMs. Human-in-the-loop is stated only as a generic principle. HYPE publishes no explainability mechanism, no named LLM provider and no scoring governance.

### Cited Findings
- Features are listed on [HYPE AI](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/ai-value-acceleration) and [HYPE enterprise AI](https://hypeinnovation.com/enterprise-ai-for-innovation-management). AI Triage is named on the idea-management page — [HYPE idea management](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/idea-management-discovery)
- On the BVW page, "AI can suggest reviewers". Submissions are checked for completeness and duplicates. AI offers savings estimation to assist reward calculations, "while people retain reward decisions" — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- Human role: AI "does not replace the human knowledge, organizational context, collaboration, and decision-making". "There is no hidden data training … Your data remains your data." Explainability is reduced to the word "transparent". "AI Use-Case Governance" appears in the navigation without detail. No LLM provider is named — [HYPE AI](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/ai-value-acceleration)
- MCP Server lets "MCP-compatible AI agents and enterprise copilots" use selected HYPE capabilities, including via Microsoft Copilot Studio. "Customer-specific LLMs" are supported through the HYPE Gen AI Pack, and Gen AI APIs are offered — [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations)
- The relaunch pitches "predictive intelligence" to "project future success" and "highly automated and autonomous" workflows — [relaunch PR](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-announces-strategic-relaunch)

### Inferences
- HYPE matches or exceeds NextUp on breadth of AI, and the MCP server plus bring-your-own LLM is strong for OEM IT. Its weak flank is the predictive/AI-estimated savings and outcome projection: there is no published statement that scores are deterministic or rule-based, and no audit trail for AI suggestions. "Scores never from model output, human Gutachter decides" is a defensible counter-position for works councils (BetrVG §87(1)6 monitoring, §90).

### Gaps
- Which LLMs are used, where inference runs (EU or not), whether AI features can be disabled per tenant, and whether AI outputs are logged. None of this is published.

## Q3. Decision workflow & accountability

### Takeaway
Configurable phase-gate workflows. Routing goes to experts, managers or committees by topic, location or category. Owners are assigned and deadlines tracked, delayed ideas are monitored, and escalation is possible. HYPE makes no public promise of a fixed answer time; SLA, reminder cadence and escalation triggers are not described.

### Cited Findings
- Ideas are routed "to experts, managers, or committees by topic, location, category, or responsibility". Owners are assigned, deadlines are tracked and delayed ideas can be monitored. Possible outcomes are approve, reject, refine, postpone and escalate, "with documented decisions". Evaluation criteria are feasibility, effort, expected savings, risk, operational impact and strategic relevance. The page is configurable to "local rules, governance models, and works council agreements" — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- "Configurable phase-gate processes", with unlimited phase-gate and agile processes — [HYPE idea management](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/idea-management-discovery)
- AI Evaluator Summary condenses ideas, attachments and discussions for evaluators — [HYPE AI](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/ai-value-acceleration)
- The Klauke case claims a "50% reduction in processing time" (vendor case) — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)

### Inferences
- HYPE has the machinery (owners, deadlines, escalation) but sells it as configurable, not as a promise. A NextUp headline such as "every idea answered in 5 working days, routed to a role" is a differentiated commitment rather than a feature gap. Whether HYPE routes to roles or to named people is not stated; the routing keys (topic, location, responsibility) suggest role-like rules are possible.

### Gaps
- Reminder and escalation automation details, and a supervisor-model (Vorgesetztenmodell) template. Neither was found.

## Q4. Impact measurement & recognition

### Takeaway
HYPE tracks savings, ROI, realised outcomes and reward value as KPIs. It supports rewards "based on implemented ideas, savings, or defined program rules", with AI savings estimation. It shows its strongest proof in big-savings case studies. No payroll or SAP-HCM premium export is documented. Points and badges are not mentioned on the current pages.

### Cited Findings
- KPIs tracked: realised cost reductions, efficiency and productivity gains, savings generated, ROI and reward value. Rewards and recognition "based on implemented ideas, savings, or defined program rules" — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- Case-study claims (vendor): ConocoPhillips over $200M/yr saved, DHL €234M, Liberty Global €18M ROI from 16k ideas, Nokia €170M new revenue, Witzenmann six-figure savings from about 1,000 ideas, OSRAM +76% cost-saving ideas and +34% savings, Klauke €120k/yr — [HYPE clients](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/en/clients); [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- The CEO wants to move "beyond simple ROI estimations to measure true impact" — [relaunch PR](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-announces-strategic-relaunch)
- SAP and Salesforce integration is only via "HYPE Custom … low-code configuration". No payroll connector is named — [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations)

### Inferences
- "Reward on measured impact" is directionally shared with HYPE. HYPE's version leans on AI-estimated savings, while NextUp's leans on measured, post-implementation impact. Prämie-to-payroll in DACH automotive is still owned by SAP-embedded BVW tools (target Idea Management), and HYPE does not visibly close that loop either.

### Gaps
- Gamification (points, badges, leaderboards) on the current product was not confirmed. Premium calculation formulas (dib-style net benefit) were not found.

## Q5. Trust, privacy & works-council readiness

### Takeaway
ISO 27001:2022 (6th year), a German HQ, GDPR support, an on-prem option and a "works council agreements" claim. HYPE publishes no hosting region or sub-processors, and no TISAX label was found. It makes no explicit "no person ranking" stance, and its AI outputs (predictive success, AI savings estimates) are the kind of thing works councils scrutinise.

### Cited Findings
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022, renewed in 2025. The old certificate scope was the ISMS of HYPE Softwaretechnik GmbH, Bonn (2013 standard) — [HYPE ISO 2025](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-renews-iso-27001-certification-for-2025); [old certificate PDF](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/hubfs/content/Certifications%20and%20similar%20docs/HYPE%20ISO%2027001%20Certificate.pdf)
- "On-premises deployment options for organizations with specific infrastructure or data residency requirements". ISO 27001 and GDPR support are mentioned. No cloud provider or region is given — [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations)
- The platform adapts to "local rules, governance models, and works council agreements" — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- "No hidden data training" — [HYPE AI](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/ai-value-acceleration)
- Viima/HYPE Boards pricing page: hosting on "secure, high-availability infrastructure", with no region stated — [Viima pricing](https://www.viima.com/pricing)
- Works councils have co-determination over the principles of the suggestion scheme (BVW) — [Rödl, Apr 2023](https://www.roedl.com/insights/praemien-ideenmanagement-steuer-rechtliche-fallgrube/)

### Inferences
- HYPE is credible on paper for DACH procurement (German GmbH, ISO 27001, on-prem). NextUp has to win on specifics: one Docker stack per company (hosted or on-prem) as the default rather than an exception, a published hosting region and sub-processors, a no-person-ranking design and deterministic scores. TISAX is open ground for both.

### Gaps
- TISAX (none found), anonymity options, data-centre region and provider, and the DPA/sub-processor list. None were found publicly.

## Q6. Integrations

### Takeaway
A strong Microsoft-centric stack: Teams, M365/SharePoint/Viva, Outlook, SSO (SAML 2.0/OIDC/OAuth 2.0), SCIM 2.0, LDAP/AD, Jira sync, Power BI/Tableau, REST/BI/Gen AI APIs, an MCP server and Copilot Studio. SAP is only "via low-code configuration".

### Cited Findings
- The full list is on [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations). Slack is not listed there; a third-party listing claims Slack and SAP — [techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/hype-innovation) (secondary)
- HYPE Boards: Jira and Teams integrations only on Pro and above, REST API and enterprise SSO (SAML, AD, Okta, Ping) only on Advanced. SCIM is not mentioned — [Viima pricing](https://www.viima.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- NextUp is unlikely to win on integration breadth. It should match the essentials (Entra SSO, SCIM, Teams, Jira, CSV/SAP export) and differentiate elsewhere.

### Gaps
- No certified SAP (HCM/payroll) connector was found.

## Q7. Pricing, deployment time, automotive customers, segment

### Takeaway
HYPE Enterprise/Innovate is quote-only and aimed at 500+ employee companies. HYPE Boards starts at $79/month (10 users). The **current** clients page shows Toyota, BMW, Volvo and Casa Pellas under automotive. Daimler, VW, Continental and Mahle (listed in the earlier note) are not on the current page; they appear only in older third-party lists. No automotive case study or quote exists apart from Casa Pellas (a Nicaraguan dealer group). Witzenmann (automotive metal-components supplier) has a BVW savings case.

### Cited Findings
- No public price, decided in a demo. "Silver Success Plan" subscriptions. HYPE "typically supports companies with 500+ employees" — [HYPE idea management](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/idea-management-discovery)
- HYPE Boards: Basic $79/mo (FAQ says €89 on monthly contracts) for 10 users and 2 boards, extra users €7/mo each. Pro (100 users, 15 boards) and Advanced (100+, unlimited) are "contact us". 14-day Pro trial — [Viima pricing](https://www.viima.com/pricing). Older third-party pricing conflicts ($39 Basic, $399 Pro, $1,799 Unlimited) — [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/viima/pricing)
- Current clients page, "Trusted by Automotive leaders": Toyota, BMW, Casa Pellas (case study PDF) and Volvo (logo only). No automotive quotes or dated cases — [HYPE clients](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/en/clients) (fetched 2 Nov 2026)
- An older third-party profile lists VW, Continental, Bosch, BASF and Airbus among 220+ customers — [siliconcanals](https://siliconcanals.com/?p=21634) (undated). The previous note listed Daimler, Volvo Group, VW, Toyota, Continental and Mahle from the same URL — `buyer_side_competitors.md`. The page has since changed.
- The BVW page shows logos for DHL, STIHL, GEA, Lufthansa and Thyssenkrupp, and cases for Witzenmann, Ricoh NZ, OSRAM, Klauke, Witt-Gruppe and Novartis — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- Relaunch flagship names: Siemens, Airbus, Novartis, TD Bank, UN, ESA — [relaunch PR](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-announces-strategic-relaunch)

### Inferences
- Treat Daimler/Mercedes, VW, Continental and Mahle as **unverified or likely former** HYPE logos as of Nov 2026. Toyota, BMW and Volvo are the logos HYPE currently claims, but no case study proves active use. HYPE's flagship proof has shifted to aerospace, pharma, banking and the public sector. That leaves room for an automotive-specialised entrant, but HYPE remains the incumbent shortlist name at German manufacturers (OSRAM, Witzenmann, STIHL, Thyssenkrupp).

### Gaps
- Deployment time: no figure found. Enterprise contract values: none found. Whether HYPE runs at Mercedes, VW, Continental or Mahle today needs direct verification (LinkedIn job posts, procurement contacts).

## Q8. What G2/Capterra reviewers praise and complain about

### Takeaway
Reviewers praise support, flexibility and the partnership. They complain about admin learning curve, complex initial setup and configuration, needing training or external help, "rigid" configuration UX and reporting.

### Cited Findings
- G2: 4.8/5 (66 reviews per search snippet). "Learning Curve" appears as a con tag on 2 reviews. Some note a learning curve for administrators and suggest improvements in reporting — [G2 via search](https://www.g2.com/products/hype-innovation-management/reviews)
- Capterra: 4.8 with about 70 reviews (Capterra CA shows 4.6 from 15, a conflicting count). Reviewers call the configuration "somewhat rigid from a user experience perspective", and say the flexibility means having "to learn and manage more" — [Capterra via search](https://www.capterra.com/p/227003/HYPE-Innovation/reviews/); [Capterra CA](https://www.capterra.ca/reviews/80591/hype-innovation)
- Infotech: "complex sometimes during initial setup", with external help and basic training needed. On a Planbox-branded review, configuring the first challenge was "more challenging than expected" — [Infotech via search](https://www.infotech.com/software-reviews/products/hype-innovation-hype-innovation?c_id=361)
- Vendor-published G2 scores: Support 97%, Ease of Setup 89% (its lowest) — [HYPE G2 PR](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/best-idea-management-software-of-spring-2025-hype-innovation-named-g2-leader)
- An earlier G2 comparison noted praise for support and drawbacks of a learning curve and complex setup — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/hype-innovation-vs-qmarkets-impact-driven-innovation-software)

### Inferences
- Setup and admin burden is HYPE's consistent weak point, which supports a NextUp "running in days, opinionated defaults" pitch. Reviewers are mostly innovation managers, not frontline employees or line managers, so the frontline experience is under-reviewed.

### Gaps
- Full review text was unavailable (G2 and Capterra returned 403). No Gartner Peer Insights data was retrieved.

## Q9. Where HYPE is weak relative to NextUp's positions (synthesis)

### Takeaway
HYPE is broad, Microsoft-integrated, ISO-certified and German, so NextUp cannot out-feature it. HYPE does not publicly commit to answer-time SLAs, deterministic or explainable scoring, role-not-person design, measured (not estimated) reward, or a per-company isolated stack as standard. Its 2026 direction (predictive AI, autonomous workflows, multinational and public-sector focus) moves it further from a works-council-safe frontline tool for automotive plants.

### Cited Findings
- No answer-time promise. Deadlines are only "tracked" — [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- AI predicts success and estimates savings — [relaunch PR](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-announces-strategic-relaunch); [HYPE BVW](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/solutions/employee-suggestion-bvw)
- On-prem is only an option "for specific requirements", and no hosting region is published — [HYPE integrations](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/integrations)
- Setup complexity complaints — see Q8

### Inferences (table-ready cells)
| Dimension | HYPE (short cell) |
|---|---|
| Capture | Web forms + AI Coach, 20+ langs, Teams embed; QR/shop-floor/anonymity not shown |
| AI | Broadest suite (coach, triage, eval summary, similarity, MCP, BYO-LLM); HITL generic, no explainability |
| Decision | Configurable phase-gates, expert/committee routing, deadlines tracked; no SLA promise |
| Impact | Savings/ROI/reward KPIs, AI savings estimate; no payroll link shown |
| Trust | ISO 27001, DE HQ, on-prem option, "works-council configurable"; no TISAX, no hosting region |
| Integrations | Teams/M365, Jira, SAML/OIDC, SCIM, Power BI, REST, MCP; SAP only via low-code |
| Price/segment | Quote-only, 500+ FTE; Boards from $79/mo; current auto logos Toyota, BMW, Volvo |
| vs NextUp | Weak on: 5-day promise, deterministic scores, role-based design, measured reward, standard isolated stack, setup effort |

### Gaps
- Live demo verification of routing-to-roles, reminders and anonymity, and of whether AI scoring can be switched off.
