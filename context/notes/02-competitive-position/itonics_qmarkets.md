# ITONICS and Qmarkets as competitors to NextUp (automotive OEM / Tier-1, DACH)

Research date: 2 Nov 2026. This pass used about 20 search/fetch calls. G2 and Capterra review pages returned HTTP 403, so the review sentiment below comes from search-result snippets of those pages, not from full review reads. Almost every feature fact comes from the vendors' own pages: **treat them as vendor claims** unless a line says otherwise. This note extends `NextUp psychographic segmentation/buyer_side_competitors.md` and does not repeat it.

## Q1. Dense feature profile per vendor (7 dimensions)

### Takeaway
ITONICS is a strategy, foresight and portfolio "Innovation OS". Its idea management is one use case among radar, roadmap and PPM, and its AI (Prism, launched Oct 2025) works at portfolio level. Qmarkets is a broader idea-management suite: Q-ideate for ideas, Q-optimize for continuous improvement/KVP, Q-scout for startup/tech scouting, Q-perform for portfolio. It has the stronger compliance story (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, 7 hosting regions incl. DE/CH, on-prem) and per-campaign AI fields (feasibility, novelty, risk). Neither publishes a response-time SLA, rewards tied to measured impact, or a TISAX label.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. Idea capture & employee effort
- ITONICS: idea capture runs through campaigns, an "idea flow board for dealer and driver input" and an "external submission portal for supplier proposals" (vendor automotive page) — [ITONICS automotive](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/automotive-industry)
- ITONICS IONX 2025 release (Sep 2025): redesigned Kanban boards, plus "ecosystem microsites", which are visual-editor templates for idea campaigns, startup intake forms and open-innovation portals. Submissions feed into governed workflows. This is a form/portal model, not guided capture — [ITONICS IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025)
- ITONICS: the AI assistant in rich-text fields has predefined actions, including translation — [ITONICS IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025)
- ITONICS: no evidence found of a shop-floor/mobile app, QR capture or a Teams app. Campaign Management and External portals are paid add-ons — [ITONICS subscription plans](https://itonics-innovation.com/knowledge/subscription-plans)
- Qmarkets: the AI Writing Assistant appears in every text box (Rewrite, Shorten, Improve Readability, Elaborate, free prompt), and AI image generation offers 4 images to choose from — [Qmarkets next-gen AI (Apr 2024)](https://qmarkets.net/resources/news/introducing-next-gen-ai-innovation-management/)
- Qmarkets: a Q-ideate integration for Microsoft Teams, built with Microsoft, embeds the whole app in Teams with SSO and sends notifications in Teams. It is described as **a charged service** — [Microsoft-hosted Qmarkets document](https://query.prod.cms.rt.microsoft.com/cms/api/am/binary/RW1lCvj) (seen via search snippet); AppSource listing [WA200007426](https://appsource.microsoft.com/product/office/WA200007426) (not opened)
- Qmarkets mobile: listed for Android, iPhone and iPad (third-party listing) — [GetApp FR](https://www.getapp.fr/software/105321/qmarkets). No QR/shop-floor kiosk capture was found on vendor pages.
- Languages: neither vendor's language count was verified in this pass (gap).

#### 2. AI assistance 2024–2026
- **ITONICS**
  - Nov 2023: launched AI capabilities in Innovation OS (already in existing notes) — [ITONICS news](https://itonics-innovation.com/news/page/4)
  - 22 Oct 2025: launched **Prism AI** for strategic portfolio decisions — [ITONICS news](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news)
  - Prism (vendor description): builds a "master context" from strategy, budgets and portfolio. It generates technology radars (dedupe, segment, rate impact/readiness, "explains its reasoning"), flags misaligned or struggling projects, finds unused budget and suggests allocations, and drafts project descriptions such as a Business Model Canvas. It runs in the customer tenant, respects roles/permissions, and the vendor says it **does not train or fine-tune models on customer data** — [ITONICS IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025)
  - Also: Nova, an AI support agent, and a Lens.org integration for patent/research intelligence — [ITONICS IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025)
  - Core tier includes the writing assistant, recommended tags/relations and duplicate detection. The signal database (500M+), recommended ratings, automated monitoring and Prism AI agents are **paid add-ons** — [ITONICS subscription plans](https://itonics-innovation.com/knowledge/subscription-plans). A third-party comparison says AI credit usage affects TCO — [rfp.wiki](https://www.rfp.wiki/project-management/innovation-management-platforms/qmarkets/itonics). The vendor plan page describes no credit system (conflict, unresolved).
  - Trust page: AI uses "state-of-the-art AI models from diligently selected vendors" (unnamed), and AI can be restricted by user role — [ITONICS Trust](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/trust)
  - Note: "**recommended ratings**" means AI suggests evaluation scores. That is the opposite of NextUp's "scores never from model output" stance.
- **Qmarkets**
  - Existing AI: similar-idea engine, automated clustering, content matchmaking, expert recommendations — [Qmarkets next-gen AI](https://qmarkets.net/resources/news/introducing-next-gen-ai-innovation-management/)
  - Apr 2024 "next-gen" AI: **Custom AI-generated fields** that admins define and AI fills, e.g. Idea Summary, Doc Summary, **Novelty Assessment, Feasibility Assessment, Security/Privacy Assessment, Risk Management**. Also the AI Writing Assistant and AI Image tool. Rolled out gradually through CSM early-adopter access — [same](https://qmarkets.net/resources/news/introducing-next-gen-ai-innovation-management/)
  - Explainability claim: "AI results are always transparent and avoiding bias", with no mechanism described. Human-in-the-loop claim: AI "can never fully replace your subject-matter experts", and add-ons are "entirely optional". The LLM provider is not disclosed — [same](https://qmarkets.net/resources/news/introducing-next-gen-ai-innovation-management/)
  - The 2024 year in review highlights AI startup scouting in Q-scout over a database of about 1M startups — [Qmarkets 2024 Rewind](https://www.qmarkets.net/resources/news/2024-rewind/) (seen via search snippet)
  - "Horizon AI" and "IRIS AI" appear in Qmarkets navigation but were not detailed — [Qmarkets next-gen AI](https://qmarkets.net/resources/news/introducing-next-gen-ai-innovation-management/)

#### 3. Decision workflow & accountability
- ITONICS: the visual process builder configures governance flows for idea/tech/trend/program management. **Each phase can have evidence requirements, gatekeepers, owners and notifications.** Classic or agile phase-gate — [ITONICS IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025). Roadmaps carry owners, dependencies and dates — [ITONICS automotive](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/automotive-industry)
- Qmarkets: role-based permissions down to individual ideas, per workspace and campaign — [Qmarkets security](https://qmarkets.net/products/security/). Hyundai Motor America testimonial: they needed a system "that could be reconfigured to match our established workflow format" — [Qmarkets customers](https://qmarkets.net/customer-success/our-customers/). Q-ideate directory listings name workflow automation — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/us/detail/q-ideate)
- Neither vendor publishes a **response-time promise to submitters** (e.g. "answer within N days"). Deadlines and reminders exist only as configurable notifications. No published SLA was found for either.

#### 4. Impact measurement & recognition
- Qmarkets **Q-optimize** is its continuous-improvement product. It covers PDCA/Kaizen/Lean/Six Sigma (blog) and offers "measure and track the ROI of your improvements over time" — [Q-optimize webinar](https://www.qmarkets.net/?p=1055185); [Qmarkets Lean Six Sigma article](https://www.qmarkets.net/resources/article/lean-six-sigma-management/)
- Qmarkets impact claims from customer testimonials (vendor page): ICL $262M realised operating-income contribution (risk-weighted >$577M anticipated); Hochschild Mining one idea ≈$40M; Swiss Post >15,000 ideas, forecast >$9.5M — [Qmarkets customers](https://qmarkets.net/customer-success/our-customers/). A Swiss Post Kaizen approach found $2.4M savings (search snippet) — [Qmarkets case studies](https://qmarkets.net/resources/case-study/)
- Qmarkets brands itself "Impact-Driven Innovation Software" (G2 product name), and its 2026 report "Die Wirkungslücke" also signals impact positioning (existing notes) — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/ideascale-vs-qmarkets-impact-driven-innovation-software)
- Qmarkets gamification (leaderboards, points, rewards) is reported only by a third-party directory, not verified on a vendor page — [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com/qmarkets)
- ITONICS: impact is portfolio-level. Toyota Motor Europe manages 200+ projects worth €20M+ — [ITONICS automotive](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/automotive-industry). Prism compares budget burn with progress — [IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025). **No gamification, points or premium (Prämie) features found** for ITONICS.
- Neither vendor was found to tie **rewards to measured impact** after implementation, or to support BVW-style Prämie calculation or payroll handoff.

#### 5. Trust, privacy, works-council readiness
- **Qmarkets** (vendor security page): **SOC 2 Type II** (audited by PwC), **ISO 27001** (all business operations), ISO 9001:2015, annual ISO audits. **7 hosting regions: US, CA, DE, CH, AU, UK, UAE**, on AWS or Azure, region fixed before implementation, storage and processing stay in region. **On-prem** on Windows/Linux on customer hardware. SAML 2.0 SSO, **SCIM**, MFA, TLS 1.2+, encryption at rest. DPA on the customer's template. **Anonymisation and pseudonymisation supported**, with defined handling of leavers' records. Optional intranet/VPN-only access. Regular pen-tests. The AI-usage guidelines policy is mentioned. **No TISAX** — [Qmarkets security](https://qmarkets.net/products/security/)
- **ITONICS**: ISO/IEC 27001:2022, certificate valid for the Nuremberg and Berlin locations, annual external audit by DEKRA — [ITONICS ISO news](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/itonics-iso-27001-re-certified); [ITONICS Trust](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/trust). Hosted on **AWS**, and **Enterprise adds regional and on-premise options** — [ITONICS subscription plans](https://itonics-innovation.com/knowledge/subscription-plans). The Trust page names no hosting location, no SOC 2, no TISAX and no sub-processors. It names an external DPO and privacy by design — [ITONICS Trust](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/trust)
- Neither vendor positions on **BetrVG/works-council** agreements, **no person ranking**, or AI-scoring governance for co-determination. Qmarkets' anonymisation feature is the closest thing (search across both sites found nothing; absence of evidence, not proof).

### Inferences
- Short table cells:
  - **Capture**: ITONICS = campaign forms/portals, desk users. Qmarkets = forms + AI writing aid, Teams embed (paid), mobile.
  - **AI**: ITONICS = portfolio AI (Prism), AI-recommended ratings. Qmarkets = AI-filled assessment fields (feasibility, novelty, risk) + similarity/clustering.
  - **Workflow**: both have configurable stage-gates with owners and notifications, and neither has a submitter-facing deadline promise.
  - **Impact**: Qmarkets Q-optimize ROI tracking. ITONICS portfolio/budget health. Neither ties rewards to measured impact.
  - **Trust**: Qmarkets SOC 2 + ISO 27001 + DE/CH region + on-prem + anonymisation. ITONICS ISO 27001 (DE sites), AWS, on-prem Enterprise-only.
  - **Integrations**: ITONICS Salesforce/Miro/Jira (3rd-party list), API Pro+. Qmarkets SAML/SCIM/Teams.
  - **Customers**: ITONICS = strongest German automotive logo wall (Daimler, Bosch, ZF, Brose, Dräxlmaier, MAN). Qmarkets = Hyundai, VW (NA), Ford (2020).

## Q2. What do G2/Capterra reviewers praise and complain about?

### Takeaway
ITONICS reviewers praise depth, flexibility and support, and complain about a steep learning curve and integration friction. Qmarkets reviewers praise configurability, an easy interface and responsive staff. Its complaints are sparse: "content more than people" and implementation effort. Review counts for both are small (~7–42), so treat the sentiment as weak signal.

### Cited Findings
- ITONICS Capterra: 4.6 (35 reviews), ease of use 4.2, customer service 4.8. An automotive innovation manager says depth brings a steep early learning curve and some advanced features need extra configuration. Another reviewer says integration with external tools could be smoother — [Capterra ITONICS](https://www.capterra.com/p/169061/ITONICS-Enterprise/reviews/) (seen via search snippet)
- ITONICS G2: 4.4 (7 reviews). Flexible and deep, but "overwhelming for newcomers". One reviewer says the learning curve is manageable thanks to support and training — [G2 ITONICS](https://www.g2.com/products/itonics-innovation-os) (seen via search snippet)
- Qmarkets Capterra: 4.8 (35 reviews), ease of use 4.7, customer service 4.8. Pros: "highly configurable software", "easy to configure", improved look and feel. Con: "more content oriented than people oriented" — [Capterra Qmarkets](https://www.capterra.com/p/83531/Qmarkets/reviews) (seen via search snippet)
- Qmarkets G2: about 4.5 (40–42 reviews, depending on the page). Reviewers praise customisation and implementation support — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/qmarkets-impact-driven-innovation-software-vs-wazoku) (seen via search snippet). HYPE vs Qmarkets G2 data is in existing notes.

### Inferences
- "Learning curve / overwhelming" (ITONICS) and "configurable" (Qmarkets) both point to admin-heavy setup. NextUp's low-effort, opinionated flow (guided capture, fixed decision promise) is a contrast that reviewers would recognise.

### Gaps
- No reviewer quotes from frontline/shop-floor users were found. Reviewers are innovation managers, which itself suggests both tools are used by innovation teams rather than by line workers.

## Q3. Where is each weak relative to NextUp's positions, and which is the better "Competitor 2/3" for an automotive comparison?

### Takeaway
On NextUp's positions, both vendors lack a submitter-facing 5-day answer promise, rewards tied to measured impact, and works-council framing. Both let AI generate ratings or assessments. Qmarkets matches or beats NextUp's hosting story (DE/CH region + on-prem + SOC 2). For an automotive comparison table, **ITONICS is the better pick for "the incumbent German innovation suite automotive already knows"** (Daimler, Bosch, ZF, Toyota, Škoda logos). **Qmarkets is the better pick for "closest feature-for-feature idea + KVP + security competitor"**. If only one slot is available next to HYPE, Qmarkets is the sharper comparison because it actually does employee idea management and continuous improvement. ITONICS mostly competes for the innovation/strategy budget, not frontline KVP.

### Cited Findings (NextUp position → vendor evidence)
- **5-day answer promise**: neither vendor states a response SLA. ITONICS offers per-phase owners and notifications — [IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025). For Qmarkets, only configurable workflows were found — [Qmarkets customers](https://qmarkets.net/customer-success/our-customers/)
- **Scores never from model output**: ITONICS sells "recommended ratings" as an AI add-on — [ITONICS plans](https://itonics-innovation.com/knowledge/subscription-plans). Qmarkets has AI-filled Feasibility/Novelty/Risk assessment fields — [Qmarkets AI](https://qmarkets.net/resources/news/introducing-next-gen-ai-innovation-management/)
- **Roles not people**: Qmarkets permissions are role-based per idea/workspace/campaign — [Qmarkets security](https://qmarkets.net/products/security/). ITONICS AI can be restricted by role — [ITONICS Trust](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/trust). Neither claims "no person ranking". Gamification leaderboards, if present at Qmarkets, are person-ranking by design — [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com/qmarkets) (third-party)
- **Reward on measured impact**: Qmarkets tracks ROI (Q-optimize) — [webinar](https://www.qmarkets.net/?p=1055185), but no link to premiums was found. ITONICS has no reward feature found.
- **One Docker stack per company, hosted or on-prem**: Qmarkets offers on-prem plus a single-region cloud — [Qmarkets security](https://qmarkets.net/products/security/). ITONICS cloud is multi-tenant (since 2016) — [IONX 2025](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/ionx-2025), with on-prem Enterprise-only — [ITONICS plans](https://itonics-innovation.com/knowledge/subscription-plans)
- **TISAX**: neither shows a TISAX label on its security/trust page — [Qmarkets security](https://qmarkets.net/products/security/); [ITONICS Trust](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/trust)

### Inferences
- NextUp's isolation story (a dedicated stack per company) is a differentiator against ITONICS' multi-tenant SaaS. Against Qmarkets it is roughly parity: Qmarkets already offers on-prem and a DE/CH region. NextUp should not lead with hosting against Qmarkets, and should instead lead with decision speed and explainable, human-only scoring.
- Qmarkets is weakest on employee effort and accountability to submitters. ITONICS is weakest on the whole frontline use case: it is built for strategy/R&D users, has paid add-ons for campaigns, and has a learning curve.
- Suggested table framing: Competitor 1 HYPE (German, broad AI, automotive logos), **Competitor 2 Qmarkets** (idea + KVP + security parity), Competitor 3 ITONICS (strategy suite, automotive logo strength) *or* the SAP-embedded BVW tool (target), depending on whether the deck targets the innovation department or the Ideenmanagement/BVW department.

### Gaps
- No evidence on win/loss between these vendors at German OEMs. It is unknown whether the ITONICS logos (Daimler, Bosch, ZF) use idea management or only foresight/roadmapping.
- The Qmarkets Volkswagen logo is tagged North America and may not be a DACH relationship. Not verified.
