# Potential offer: future NextUp benefits beyond PILOT (1–3 year horizon, as of Nov 2026)

Scope: benefits NextUp does not provide today and that are not in the PILOT Hauptplan, which could extend the offer for large DACH automotive companies (OEMs, large Tier-1s). Part 1 covers NextUp-internal ideas (repos and vault, read-only). Part 2 covers external 2025–2027 trends. Effort figures are **my estimates** (S = days to 2 weeks, M = 2–6 weeks, L = 2–4 months for the current 4-person team, XL = more than that or needs partners). They do not come from any source.

## External: which 2025–2027 trends support new benefits for automotive buyers?

### Takeaway
The strongest external tailwinds are (a) agentic AI moving from "assist" to "do" inside enterprise apps and quality workflows (8D drafting, copilots in Teamcenter/Polarion/Opcenter), (b) EU AI Act/BetrVG obligations that make explainable, works-council-ready AI a buying criterion (the high-risk Annex III deadline moved to 2 Dec 2027, but Art. 26(7) worker-information duties and German co-determination still apply), (c) German idea-management benchmarking data showing measurable ROI and strong GenAI interest, and (d) frontline/voice capture where phones are restricted. Catena-X is growing fast, but the evidence for idea sharing across suppliers on it is thin. It is a data-exchange network for quality/traceability, not for ideas.

### Cited Findings
**Agentic AI that implements (8D, tickets, copilots)**
- Gartner (26 Aug 2025) predicts 40 % of enterprise apps will feature task-specific AI agents by 2026, up from <5 % in 2025. — [Gartner press release](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-08-26-gartner-predicts-40-percent-of-enterprise-apps-will-feature-task-specific-ai-agents-by-2026-up-from-less-than-5-percent-in-2025)
- An IEOM 2026 paper proposes an LLM + RAG "AI-augmented 8D" framework, comparing automotive practice (IATF 16949, VDA 6.3). — [IEOM 2026 proceedings](https://ieomsociety.org/proceedings/milwaukee2026/191.pdf)
- A 2026 IIETA systematic review (PRISMA, 47 articles) found only 6 directly on AI in 8D, so the field is early. — [IIETA](https://www.iieta.org/node/24363)
- An OST (Eastern Switzerland UAS) thesis generated 8D reports with GPT-4o-mini + RAG and a self-check that flags missing fields. — [OST eprints](https://eprints.ost.ch/id/eprint/1324/)
- Vendor claims (unverified): DeepQMS −70 % 8D creation/validation effort after a pilot ([all-about-industries](https://www.all-about-industries.com/ai-detect-quality-issues-early-8d-report-a-79fb12deb6045c8f02e9a7a313083711/)); a Chinese multi-agent quality system with a dedicated 8D agent, +30 % engineer efficiency in one case ([china.com](https://m.tech.china.com/hea/articles/20260413/202604131845770.html)).
- Siemens announced at CES 2026 nine new AI copilots, including for Teamcenter, Polarion and Opcenter ([CIMdata summary](https://www.cimdata.com/de/industry-summary-articles/item/29194-siemens-unveils-technologies-to-accelerate-the-industrial-ai-revolution-at-ces-2026)). It also plans an industrial AI agent marketplace open to third-party agents ([control.com](https://control.com/news/ai-agents-from-siemens-tackle-labor-gaps-in-industry/)).
- Idea-management vendors are adding AI. HYPE markets AI agents to "discover insights, develop ideas, and scale execution" ([HYPE](https://hypeinnovation.com/enterprise-ai-for-innovation-management)). ITONICS has a chat co-pilot that drafts ideas and ratings ([ITONICS blog](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/blog/ai-innovation-management-tools)). **AI Act / explainability / works council**
- Digital Omnibus: standalone Annex III high-risk obligations (which include employment/HR uses) move from 2 Aug 2026 to **2 Dec 2027**, and Annex I embedded systems to 2 Aug 2028. Provisional agreement 7 May 2026; Council approval reported 29 Jun 2026. Official Journal publication was not confirmed in these sources. — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement); [SecurePrivacy](https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-the-new-high-risk-ai-deadlines-after-council-approval); [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/The-Digital-AI-Omnibus-Proposed-deferral-of-high-risk-AI-obligations-under-the-AI-Act)
- Art. 26(7): a deployer using a high-risk AI system at work must inform workers' representatives and affected workers before use. A Deloitte deck ties this in Germany to BetrVG §87(1) Nos. 1 and 6. — [AI Act Art. 26](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/26/); [Deloitte Legal](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/dl/Documents/legal/Deloitte%20Legal%20Webcast%2006.11.2024%20(EN).pdf)
- Commentary (July 2026): the deferral does not remove the duty to consult workers under national law and collective agreements. — [Shaping Tomorrow scan](https://decision-intel.shapingtomorrow.com/scans/workforce-skills-organisational-change/2026-07-11-algorithmic-codetermination/scan.html)
