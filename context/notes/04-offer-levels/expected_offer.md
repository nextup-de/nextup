# Expected offer: what large DACH buyers (automotive OEM / Tier-1) take for granted in idea-management / BVW / KVP software (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: Kotler "expected product" layer for NextUp. Competitor profiles (HYPE, ITONICS, Qmarkets, SAP-based and light rivals) are already in `research_notes/NextUp competitive position analysis/` and are not repeated here. Research ran ~15 tool calls; most primary material is vendor pages and anonymised tender summaries on softguide.de, so treat vendor claims as claims.

## 1. What German tenders / RFPs for Ideenmanagement software require (product features)

### Takeaway
Across the German tenders and selection checklists found, the recurring "must-haves" are: simple (mobile) submission, configurable evaluation/approval workflow with expert reviewers (Gutachter) and committee, premium calculation inside the tool (formula/table), status transparency for the submitter, reminders/escalation, archive + full-text search + duplicate check, KPI reporting/statistics, multi-language, roles & rights, SSO/API/import-export, and (for SAP shops) HR-master-data and payroll integration. The works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung) is the frame the software must be configured to, not an optional extra.

### Cited Findings
**Tender examples (softguide.de Ausschreibungen; all marked "beendet", years ~2017-2018, anonymised)**
- Global industrial company (paints/chemicals), ~5,000 users, pilot in Switzerland then rollout Europe/Asia/America: worldwide submission by any employee, central idea database, evaluation by responsible persons with status tracking, status visible to employees "via dashboard or app", visual concept-to-implementation management, rating + reward + recognition, fast feedback, access to existing ideas, statistics and decision support, reporting, images/video, interfaces to other apps (e.g. learning systems, SharePoint), web-based, smartphone/tablet/interactive monitor/desktop, German + English — [softguide.de tender 17/788](https://www.softguide.de/ausschreibungen/industrieunternehmen-sucht-software-fuer-das-ideenmanagement)
- Consumer-goods producer, ~5,000 staff, BVW initially Germany only: traceable status per idea, search/filter to check existing ideas, archive with full-text search and tagging, further development of ideas on the platform, ~5 key users with decision rights + ~2,000 users, evaluation and premium/award handling, statistics, automatic e-mails and reminder mails for overdue suggestions, multilingual (DE/EN/FR), iOS/Android app, "simple to use", modern look; vendor must itemise one-time licence vs. recurring costs (phone support, updates, maintenance) — [softguide.de tender 18/1239](https://www.softguide.de/ausschreibungen/renommiertes-produktions-und-handelsunternehmen-sucht-ideenmanagement)
- Electro/metal industrial company, ~800 employees: committee decision on implementation based on the expert's (Gutachter) assessment, and "calculation of the premium via the software" — [softguide.de tender ~800 MA](https://www.softguide.de/ausschreibungen/industrieunternehmen-sucht-software-fuer-das-ideenmanagement-ca-800); English version [softguide.com](https://www.softguide.com/it-projects/industrial-company-seeks-idea-management-software-for-approximately-800)

**Market-standard function catalogue (SoftGuide "typical functions" of KVP/BVW software, page © 2026)**
- Access/usability: desktop/tablet/smartphone; mapping of the org structure; simple submission; online help; multilingual + translation functions; corporate design; **single sign-on and API access** — [SoftGuide Funktionen KVP/BVW](https://www.softguide.de/software-tipps/funktionen-tipps-kvp-bvw-software)
- Process: idea-management and innovation process mapping, stage-gate, workflow management, milestones, **reminder and escalation** — same source
- Capture: **automatic duplicate checking**, tagging/categorisation, drafts, image/video/PDF upload, **deputy (Vertreter) rules**, campaigns/competitions — same source
- Evaluation: custom criteria, prioritisation, review/assessment process, **custom scoring forms with automatic calculation**, transparent award process — same source
- Engagement: gamification, incentive/reward system, likes/following, surveys, news — same source
- Reporting/admin: archiving & backup, user-defined KPIs, reporting/statistics, mail merge/form letters (e.g. premium letters), import/export interfaces, **role and rights management, multi-client capability** — same source

**Vendor checklist for the selection process (HYPE, Feb 2022, German)**
- Five steps: visualise the process with all decision paths, roles and loops; map framework conditions in a **Betriebsvereinbarung** (send existing BV to vendors; if none, write one); design premium/premium calculation individually ("via formulas, tables"; how much is paid out; further incentives); transparency via reporting (which KPIs for which audience, e.g. top management); what eases daily work (onboarding for users, continuous training on updates, access to experienced domain experts) — [HYPE Checkliste Softwareauswahlprozess (PDF)](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/hubfs/checkliste.pdf)

**SAP-centred expectation (target Idea Management, used by large German industrials)**
- Uses SAP HR and org-management data directly (SAP ERP HCM/OM; cloud variant for SuccessFactors), **rewards transferred directly to SAP payroll**, customizable reviewer/expert forms with benefit analyses, worklists, 60+ search filters, >50 preconfigured KPIs with drill-down by SAP org chart and Excel export, 8 UI languages + translation tool, idea similarity search, points account / rewards shop, mobile, automated reminders and deadline checks; on-prem on SAP NetWeaver with a hybrid cloud add-on; ">100 organisations worldwide" — [TIMETOACT target Idea Management On-Premise](https://www.timetoact-group.com/en/details/target-idea-management-on-premise); cloud listing [SAP Store](https://www.sap.com/swiss/products/hcm/partners/wca-walldorf-consulting-gmbh-target-idea-management-cloud.html)

**Works council (Betriebsrat) frame**
- § 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 12 BetrVG gives the works council co-determination over the principles of the betriebliches Vorschlagswesen — [Haufe: BR-Mitbestimmung betriebliches Vorschlagswesen](https://www.haufe.de/id/beitrag/br-mitbestimmung-betriebliches-vorschlagswesen-HI3548248.html); scope vs. systematic Ideenmanagement discussed in [Rieble/Gistel, ZAAR](https://www.zaar.uni-muenchen.de/pub/vr2005-6.pdf)
- Under § 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6 BetrVG, software that is *capable* of collecting behaviour/performance data triggers co-determination, with no de-minimis threshold; BAG 29.09.2020 (1 ABR 32/19) treats software introduction and updates as co-determination-relevant — [Luther: Software vs. Mitbestimmung § 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6](https://www.luther-lawfirm.com/en/newsroom/blog/detail/dauerbrenner-software-vs-mitbestimmung-87-abs-1-nr-6-betrvg); AI-specific: [CMS: IT-Mitbestimmung und KI](https://cms.law/en/deu/legal-updates/it-mitbestimmung-und-ki)
- WEKA guidance on how the works council co-determines Ideenmanagement — [WEKA](https://www.weka.de/betriebsrat-personalrat/ideenmanagement-so-bestimmt-der-betriebsrat-mit/)

**KPI expectations (what reporting must be able to produce)**
- Standard KPI definitions for DACH idea management were set by Zentrum Ideenmanagement (ZI, dib) in 2012 — [Viessmann: comparison of key figures 2020](https://www.viessmann-climatesolutions.com/en/newsroom/company/comparison-of-key-figures-in-idea-management-2020.html)
- Neckel benchmark (252 DACH companies): avg. 321 ideas per 1,000 employees/year, avg. implementation ratio 44.4% (vendor summary of the report) — [Qmarkets summary of benchmark](https://qmarkets.net/resources/article/continuous-improvement-tools-and-benchmarks/); 2020 edition: 196 companies, 1.33 M employees, 256,000 ideas — [Viessmann](https://www.viessmann-climatesolutions.com/en/newsroom/company/comparison-of-key-figures-in-idea-management-2020.html)
- rready (2026 blog): "Centralized idea submission is a given"; most platforms share similar core features, value lies in execution; lists submission, collaboration, voting, campaigns, gamification, reporting, RBAC, search/organisation, AI, integrations (incl. Azure/SSO) — [rready: top features](https://www.rready.com/blog/top-features-of-idea-management-software)

### Inferences
- Expected (table stakes) feature set for a DACH BVW/KVP buyer: (1) low-friction submission on desktop + mobile, incl. drafts, attachments, team ideas; (2) configurable multi-step workflow: line manager → Gutachter/expert → committee (Bewertungskommission) → implementation, with deputies, deadlines, reminders and escalation; (3) **premium calculation in the tool** (formula/table: savings-based and non-quantifiable points tables), letters, and hand-off to payroll (SAP); (4) submitter status transparency; (5) archive with full-text search and duplicate/similarity check; (6) ZI-style KPI reporting (participation rate, ideas/100 employees, implementation rate, cycle time, savings, premiums) with org-unit drill-down and Excel export; (7) multilingual UI; (8) roles/rights, multi-site/multi-client; (9) SSO, HR master-data import, API/import-export; (10) configurability to the Betriebsvereinbarung (process rules, premium rules, visibility/pseudonymisation of submitters and reviewers, no individual performance tracking without agreement).
- NextUp's AI analysis/discussion is *not* what tenders ask for; tenders ask for workflow, premium and reporting. If NextUp lacks premium calculation, Gutachter workflow with deadlines/escalation, payroll export and ZI KPIs, it will fail the expected layer in a BVW tender regardless of AI.
- Works-council fit is an expected element in DACH: buyers will assume the vendor can configure anonymity/pseudonymity and data minimisation and supply documentation for the BR (data-flow, which data is logged, what managers can see).

### Gaps
- No current (2024-2026) public tender for Ideenmanagement software was found on TED / bund.de in this pass; softguide tenders are older (2017-2018) and anonymised. Automotive OEM tenders are run privately (e.g. via SAP Ariba) and are not public.
- No Zentrum Ideenmanagement / dib software-requirements checklist was found online; the ZI KPI definitions themselves were not retrieved.
- Anonymous submission as an explicit tender requirement was not found in the tenders read (it appears as a BV topic, not documented here with a source).

## 2. Service expectations (implementation, training, support, hosting, security, pricing, export)

### Takeaway
Buyers expect: onboarding + ongoing training and access to domain experts (Ideenmanagement consulting), itemised licence vs. recurring service costs (support, updates, maintenance), EU/DE hosting or on-prem/SAP-hosted option, ISO 27001 as the security baseline (TISAX is common in the automotive supply chain but no idea-management vendor was found advertising it), SSO, data deletion at contract end, and quote-based annual licences. Published uptime SLAs are *not* standard among incumbents, so a written SLA is a possible differentiator rather than an expectation that rivals meet.

### Cited Findings
- HYPE checklist explicitly frames onboarding for users, continuous training on updates and access to experienced experts (in person) as selection criteria — [HYPE checklist PDF](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/hubfs/checkliste.pdf)
- Tender 18/1239 required the vendor to split one-time product price vs. recurring service costs (phone support, updates, maintenance contracts) — [softguide.de](https://www.softguide.de/ausschreibungen/renommiertes-produktions-und-handelsunternehmen-sucht-ideenmanagement)
- ITONICS: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified since 2017, recertified; certificate covers German sites Nuremberg and Berlin; customer data deleted at end of contract — [ITONICS Trust](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/trust); [ITONICS ISO 27001 recertification](https://www.itonics-innovation.com/news/itonics-iso-27001-recertification)
- target Idea Management runs on-prem on the customer's SAP NetWeaver or as cloud on SAP's platform — [TIMETOACT](https://www.timetoact-group.com/en/details/target-idea-management-on-premise); [TIMETOACT cloud](https://www.timetoact-group.com/en/details/target-idea-management-cloud)
- German B2B SaaS vendors serving automotive advertise "hosting in Germany, data in Germany, ISO 27001 + TISAX" as a trust package (e.g. movexm, joomo; not idea-management vendors) — [movexm security](https://movexm.com/en/why-movexm/security-and-data-protection); [joomo hosting](https://joomo.de/en/produkte-und-services/hosting-cloudloesungen-und-benutzerverwaltung)
- Qmarkets: quote-based annual licences (Core / Enterprise / Enterprise-Strategic Innovation, prices undisclosed); G2 aggregated implementation time ~4 months; no prominently published uptime SLA / status page verified; integrations and change-management services add to contract value — [rfp.wiki Qmarkets](https://www.rfp.wiki/project-management/innovation-management-platforms/qmarkets); [PricingSaaS Qmarkets](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/qmarkets); [G2 Qmarkets pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/qmarkets-innovation-management/pricing)
- rready lists RBAC and Azure/SSO integration as standard security elements and links a "Data Security and Sovereignty" trust centre — [rready](https://www.rready.com/blog/top-features-of-idea-management-software)
- SoftGuide catalogue counts archiving & backup and import/export interfaces as typical functions (i.e. data export expected) — [SoftGuide](https://www.softguide.de/software-tipps/funktionen-tipps-kvp-bvw-software)

### Inferences
- Expected service layer for a DACH OEM/Tier-1: German/EU hosting with DPA (AVV) and sub-processor list; ISO 27001 (own or via hosting partner, with certificate scope stated); TISAX at least on the roadmap for automotive (idea data includes product/process know-how); SSO (Entra ID/SAML); on-prem or customer-cloud option for SAP-heavy groups; German-language support and handbook; onboarding + admin/Gutachter training; BR documentation pack; full data export and deletion at contract end; annual licence priced per employee/user or per site with implementation services itemised.
- Typical incumbent implementation is months (G2 ~4 months for Qmarkets). A fast (weeks) go-live and a written uptime SLA would sit in the *augmented* layer, since incumbents do not visibly offer them.
- NextUp's "one Docker stack per company, hosted VM or on-prem" fits the expected on-prem/EU-hosting demand; lacking ISO 27001/TISAX would be a gap in the expected layer for OEM security reviews.

### Gaps
- No idea-management vendor was found advertising TISAX; whether OEM security reviews actually demand TISAX for an idea tool (vs. ISO 27001 + DPA) is unconfirmed.
- No public per-user prices or uptime SLA figures for HYPE, ITONICS, Qmarkets, target were found; G2 implementation figure is an aggregate of reviewers, not a vendor commitment.
- Support SLA norms (response times) for this category not found.

## 3. Which AI features became table stakes since 2024 (no longer differentiating)

### Takeaway
Semantic duplicate/similarity detection, AI summaries for evaluators, automatic translation, AI-assisted idea writing/coaching, tagging/clustering and AI scoring are now advertised by essentially every relevant vendor (HYPE, Ideanote, IdeaScale, Ideawake, rready, Crowdworx, Aha!, and even SAP-based target has similarity search). These are expected; differentiation has moved to how AI is governed (no training on customer data, MCP/connected context, explainability, BR-compatibility) and to AI that drives decisions end-to-end.

### Cited Findings
- HYPE AI suite: Innovation Graph, AI Coach, AI Idea Generator, AI Evaluator Summary (condenses idea, attachments, discussion for evaluators), Similarity Search (duplicates), Campaign Assistant, AI Trend Generator, Web Clipper, Startup Scouting; "There is no hidden data training"; supports MCP — [HYPE AI](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/ai-value-acceleration); AI Triage flags duplicates/incomplete entries — [HYPE Enterprise AI](https://hypeinnovation.com/enterprise-ai-for-innovation-management); HYPE relaunch as "AI-first data platform" — [HYPE news](https://www.hypeinnovation.com/news/hype-innovation-announces-strategic-relaunch)
- Ideanote: embedding on every submission, cross-language semantic duplicate detection (French idea matches Japanese), merge keeps comments/votes/credit; plus AI scoring, sentiment analysis, illustration — [Ideanote duplicate detection](https://ideanote.io/feature/ai-duplicate-detection); [Ideanote AI scoring](https://ideanote.io/feature/ai-idea-scoring)
- IdeaScale: duplicate detection at submission — [IdeaScale help](https://help.ideascale.com/idea-duplicate-detection-tool); Ideawake: automatic duplicate detection and merging — [Ideawake AI](https://ideawake.com/product/artificial-intelligence/); Aha! Ideas: AI duplicate detection launched Nov 2024 (Advanced plan) — [Aha! blog](https://www.aha.io/blog/just-launched-identify-duplicate-customer-ideas-with-ai)
- Crowdworx: automatic translation of ideas, comments, evaluations and decisions — [Crowdworx 2025 platforms](https://www.crowdworx.com/resources/the-most-comprehensive-idea-management-platforms-for-2025)
- rready (2026): AI clustering of similar ideas, evaluation support, helping users structure input, next-step recommendations, smarter search; says AI is becoming a baseline — [rready](https://www.rready.com/blog/top-features-of-idea-management-software)
- SoftGuide's generic KVP/BVW function list already includes "automatic duplicate checking" and "translation functions" as *typical* functions — [SoftGuide](https://www.softguide.de/software-tipps/funktionen-tipps-kvp-bvw-software)
- target (SAP-based) offers idea similarity search — [TIMETOACT](https://www.timetoact-group.com/en/details/target-idea-management-on-premise)
- Counterpoint: a 2026 buyer's guide still calls duplicate detection, auto-scoring and trend analysis "a meaningful differentiator between platforms" (vendor-authored listicle) — via [Crowdworx](https://www.crowdworx.com/resources/the-most-comprehensive-idea-management-platforms-for-2025) / search summary; reviewer note that duplicate-detection quality depends on configuration and data hygiene — [rfp.wiki HYPE](https://www.rfp.wiki/project-management/innovation-management-platforms/hype-innovation)
- Works council: AI features fall under IT co-determination (§ 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6 BetrVG) — [CMS: IT-Mitbestimmung und KI](https://cms.law/en/deu/legal-updates/it-mitbestimmung-und-ki)

### Inferences
- Expected AI (no longer differentiating for NextUp): duplicate/similarity detection (incl. cross-language), summaries for reviewers, machine translation of ideas/comments, AI help to write/structure a submission, auto-tagging/clustering, basic AI scoring.
- Still potentially differentiating (augmented/potential layer): AI that grounds analysis in company data with visible sources ("checked across the company"), turns an idea into a decision-ready case/project plan, explains scoring transparently enough for a BR and a Gutachter to accept it, and runs on-prem/EU with no training on customer data. HYPE already claims "no hidden data training" and MCP, so those alone are also edging into expected.
- Presence of AI is assumed; *quality* (precision of duplicate matching in German shop-floor language, DE/EN/CZ/HU/PL translation for plants) and governance are what buyers will test.

### Gaps
- No independent benchmark of AI duplicate-detection accuracy or translation quality found.
- No DACH tender text found that explicitly requires AI features; the table-stakes judgement rests on vendor feature saturation, not buyer requirement documents.
- Could not confirm release dates for HYPE's individual AI features (2024 vs 2025).
