# NextUp training: 30 minutes for staff, desk holders and managers

> **Updated 2026-11-04.** For the pilot lead to run, in person or on a call, in the week the company goes to pilot. The
> German version is `TRAINING.de.md`; both say the same. Show the real app of your company in sandbox; use a test case,
> not a real one. No slides are needed.

**Goal:** after 30 minutes everyone knows how to raise something, the desk holders know how to answer on time, everyone
knows what the deputy does and which reminders to expect, and nobody thinks NextUp measures people.

## Agenda

| Min | What | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | Why: problems and ideas that wait too long | everyone |
| 3 to 11 | Raise something | everyone |
| 11 to 19 | Answer on time | desk holders and managers |
| 19 to 23 | The deputy | everyone, short for staff |
| 23 to 26 | Reminders and mail | everyone |
| 26 to 28 | What is counted, and what is not | everyone |
| 28 to 30 | Questions, who to ask | everyone |

## 1. Why (3 minutes)

People have ideas and see problems, and most never get an answer. NextUp makes a promise: a case that is raised gets
an answer in a set number of working days (your company set it; five unless told otherwise). Everyone can see who the
case is with and by when an answer is due. That is the whole idea.

## 2. Raise something (8 minutes) — everyone

1. Sign in with your company account (or your login code).
2. Open the raise page and describe the problem or the idea in your own words. Short is fine: what is wrong or what you
   would change, and where.
3. NextUp proposes where it goes, the role that owns this kind of case. With the AI off the proposal comes from keywords.
   Check it. If it is wrong, choose another role; your choice wins and is recorded as yours.
4. Send. You see the case, who it is with, and the day an answer is due.
5. Your drafts are private. Only the people who need the case to answer it see it.

Practice: everyone raises one test case now. Say out loud who it went to.

## 3. Answer on time (8 minutes) — desk holders and managers

A desk is a role such as "team lead assembly". Whoever sits at the desk gets the cases routed to it.

1. A new case appears in your inbox (and, in the pilot, as a mail). The case page says **Answer by** and how many
   working days are left.
2. Answer in one of four ways: **Yes**; **Yes, as a project**; **Ask a question** back to the person who raised it; or
   **No**. Give a short reason, especially for a No: a reasoned answer is what the person is waiting for.
3. A question counts as a first response and keeps the promise. It does not replace the decision: when the person
   answers, the case is back with you.
4. Your answer goes into the decision log. People who need to know can read it there.
5. If you cannot answer because it is not yours, move it to the right role instead of leaving it.

Managers also see the dashboard and the weekly pilot report, in numbers by company, unit and role (see 6).

Practice: the desk holder in the room answers the test case with a Yes and one with a No and a reason.

## 4. The deputy (4 minutes)

Every desk has a deputy. When the answer date passes with no answer, the deputy is told and, depending on the case, it is
handed to them; the case page says which, and when. This is not a punishment. It is so that a case never waits for a
person on holiday or sick.

- Desk holders: tell your deputy when you are away for a longer time. Check that the deputy is a person who can decide.
- Staff: you do not need to chase; the case page shows who has it now.

## 5. Reminders and mail (3 minutes)

- In the pilot a mail goes to the role that owns a case when it is raised, and to the people it concerns when it moves on or is answered.
- Reminders before the answer date and a daily summary go out only if your company switched them on. The daily summary is
  off for each person until they turn it on in their notification settings.
- In sandbox nothing is sent. Do not be surprised in the training: the app is in sandbox until the switch.
- Mails are short and link to the case. NextUp never asks you for a password by mail.

## 6. What is counted, and what is not (2 minutes)

NextUp counts cases, answers and working days per company, per unit and per role. It never ranks a person, never shows a
list of the "fastest" or "slowest" people, and does not count what one person does. A unit with fewer than five people
is hidden in the report so nobody can be picked out. The pilot report is for judging whether the process works, not
whether you do.

## 7. Questions and help (2 minutes)

- A question about how to use it: your pilot lead.
- Something broken: the problem button in the app, or the contact the pilot lead names in `docs/pilot/SUPPORT.md`.
- Your works agreement and data protection papers: your works council and data protection officer.

## For the trainer

- Do the training in sandbox with test cases; delete none, they stay in the log.
- Have a manager, a desk holder and a normal member signed in on three screens or three accounts, so everyone sees all
  three views.
- If someone asks something you cannot answer, write it down and ask NextUp. Do not guess about data protection.
