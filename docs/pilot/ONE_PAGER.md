## The problem

Ideas and problems sit in a mailbox or a hallway for weeks. Nobody knows who was asked, whether a decision was made or why.
People stop raising things.

## What NextUp does

A person raises a problem or an idea. NextUp sends it to the role that owns that kind of case. The role answers within a
promise: five working days unless you set another. If the answer date passes, the role's deputy is told or takes over. Every
decision is written down with its reason where those who need it can read it. A weekly report shows, against the time
before, how fast and how reliably cases get answered.

It works with the AI switched off. The AI is optional and is off in the pilot unless you decide otherwise.

## What the pilot includes

| Part | What you get |
|---|---|
| A stack of your own | One NextUp installation for your company on its own server, hosted by NextUp or on your server. Nothing is shared with another customer. |
| Your people and structure | Imported from your files, or read from Microsoft Entra ID or SAP SuccessFactors. NextUp only reads, and only the fields named in the handouts. |
| Sign-in | Your Microsoft work accounts (single sign-on), or login codes. |
| German and English | The screens and the mails, per person. |
| The case loop | Raise, route, answer, decide; a working-day calendar for your sites; deputies; reminders when you switch them on. |
| Setting it up together | One joint session of about half a day with NextUp, your IT and your pilot lead. Your admin moves the company from sandbox to pilot. |
| Training | 30 minutes for staff, desk holders and managers, in German and English. |
| The weekly report | Cases per 10 people, first answer, answered on time and use, against a baseline. Units under 5 people are hidden. |
| Data protection papers | Drafts to read and adapt with your own legal people: data processing agreement, technical measures, record of processing, impact assessment, works agreement template. They are not signed and not legally reviewed. |
| Support | On working days, through a mailbox and a weekly call. You grant NextUp access to your data only for a time you choose. |

## What it does not do

- It does not rank or count people. No person numbers, anywhere.
- It does not read your mail, calendars, chats or files, and it never writes to your directory.
- No certification (TISAX, ISO 27001), no 24/7 operation, no high availability, no outside penetration test yet.
- Further channels and connectors (Teams, shop-floor access, Jira, Microsoft 365 search) are not part of this offer. They
  can be discussed once the core pilot runs.
