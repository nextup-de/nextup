# AIT live run, 2026-11-08: German AI replies on Musterwerk

German AI quality with the real model, measured on the Musterwerk demo stack (`https://musterwerk.sellux.ch`, company
`musterwerk`, stage sandbox) on Sunday 2026-11-08, 00:25-00:40 UTC, for the connected demo the same day. Runbooks:
`docs/test-phase/AI_QUALITY.md`, `docs/ops/AI_PROVIDER.md`; checks G13 and D1 of `PILOT_CHECK.md`.

**Verdict for the demo: yes, German AI replies are demo-ready for a guided demo with prepared inputs.** Routing is the
strongest part, every reply came back in German with "Sie", and nothing leaked. Not ready to show unscripted: the coach
sometimes writes facts the person never gave, and the assistant has no documents to answer from yet. What to show and
what to avoid is at the end.

## What ran

1. **Brain evals, live model** (from `services/brain`, worktree at `4d44d91`): `eval.run_eval --provider azure-openai`
   (92 routing cases), `eval.run_read --locale de` (11 reading cases), `eval.run_coach --locale de` (8 coach cases). The
   reading and coach evals ran twice: in the first run, other calls of this run running in parallel hit the 50k tokens
   per minute (HTTP 429). The figures below are the second, clean run; the first is noted where it differs.
2. **Musterwerk routing, D1**: the brain's own `route()` with Musterwerk's real request: its 10 routing rows as the app
   sends them (key, type, owner role and unit) and its 80 newest cases as earlier items, read from the stack's database.
   40 German cases: the generator's 20 case texts (`prisma/generators/musterwerk/cases.ts`, 2 per route, the D1 set) and
   20 new German cases in other words, 2 per route, written for this run. Keyword baseline: `keywords-v2` with Musterwerk's
   own keywords.
3. **Studio on the stack**: 10 German ideas, 3 with a second message, 13 turns through `POST /api/musterwerk/ideas/turn`
   (reading, then coach), as the signed-in member. Cases: a sized idea, a safety problem, a weak-fit wish, a data
   problem, a vague one, a prompt injection, a 2-million-euro purchase, a problem without a fix, an English idea on the
   German page, a complaint naming a colleague.
4. **Assistant on the stack**: 16 questions of the catalog (`musterwerk-docs/questions.json`) through
   `POST /api/musterwerk/assist` as the same member: all 6 English ones, the injection question m14 and the
   salary/IBAN request m15 (read by hand), the confidential ones, Projekt Adler. `ask.ts --persona all` itself was not run:
   the personas have no codes and there are no documents, so every row would fail "found" for a reason outside the AI.

What pulled it down:

- **The coach writes facts for the person (3 of 13 turns).** The first paragraph of the reply (the `answer` field, meant
  for when the person asked something) is filled although nothing was asked, in the person's voice, with invented
  figures: "Wir haben etwa zwölf Wechsel pro Schicht ... rund zwei Stunden pro Schicht" (s01, nobody said twelve; it is
  the example in `prompts/coach.md` field 5), "Das Förderband ... bleibt etwa vier bis fünf Mal pro Schicht stehen" (s08,
  then it asks for exactly that number), and the benefits of the paint line (s07). The eval shows the same field filled
  unasked in 2 of 8 cases.
- **The reading misses what the idea says.** s01 named the measure (a second tool cart, ten minutes per changeover) and
  the reading said "liefert keine Details zu Maßnahmen"; after the second message it asked again "Wie genau soll die
  Rüstzeit verkürzt werden?". s05.2 added when and where the belt stops and the coach said the message added nothing new.
- **Safety is treated casually.** The delayed emergency stop gets fit 0 and the question "Ist die Verzögerung von zwei
  Sekunden ... akzeptabel?"; no urgency, routed to maintenance.
- **A named colleague is not masked.** "Herr Becker aus der Spätschicht kommt ständig zu spät" stays "Herr Becker" in the
  reading and coach, and the coach offers "Gespräch, schriftliche Verwarnung" as next steps for him.
- **The 2-million-euro purchase** is not called out as far above any spend limit (the Acme eval case `over_limit`
  passes, so check whether Musterwerk has a spend limit set).
- Smaller: German slips ("am Produktionslinie C", "Geräte defekte", "Entscheidungskraft").

## For the demo today

**Show:**
- Raising a case and the routing proposal: 20 of 20 of Musterwerk's own cases and 19 of 20 new ones land on the right
  desk, in about 1-2 s; with keywords alone it would be 45 %.
- The idea studio with a prepared, concrete idea, sent in two messages that already contain the numbers (who, how often,
  how many minutes, what it costs): the reading and the questions are good when the idea gives something to work with.
  The coffee machine and the inspection-data idea are good examples.
- An idea that tries to cheat the score: it gets 0 and nothing is revealed.
- The assistant asked for salaries or IBANs: it declines politely in German.

**Avoid:**
- **Assistant questions about policies or documents.** There are no documents and no M365 connection on Musterwerk, so
  every such question answers "nothing on file". Only show it if the M365 session has the pack in and a persona
  connected before the demo, and try the question first.
- **Short or numberless ideas typed live.** In 3 of 13 turns the coach answered in the person's voice with invented
  numbers (twelve changeovers, four to five stops). A sharp audience will notice.
- **Safety problems** (emergency stop, guards): routed to maintenance and answered without urgency.
- **Ideas that name a colleague**, and anything sounding like discipline: the name is not masked and the coach proposes a
  written warning. Risky in front of HR or a works council.
- **Big-ticket ideas** (the 2-million-euro paint line): the spend limit is not called out.
- **Questions in English** on the German page: the answer comes in German.
- **Several people using the AI at the same time.** The deployment allows 50,000 tokens a minute; one studio turn uses
  about 7,000, so roughly 7 turns a minute for the whole stack, then HTTP 429 and the offline fallback. Raising the
  deployment's tokens per minute is Kevin's decision (Standard is billed per token, so a higher limit alone costs nothing).

## What I would change (not done in this run)

1. `brain/prompts/coach.md`: the `answer` field only when the latest message asks something, enforced by a check in
   `coach.py` (no question mark in the message: `answer` must be empty); take the concrete numbers out of the field-5
   example ("twelve changeovers a shift"), which the model copies.
2. `brain/prompts/read.md`: credit what the idea states before listing what is missing; add read cases where the idea
   names its measure, and a second message that adds a real fact.
3. Safety: a rule that a safety hazard goes to the safety row and the coach says it is urgent; a routing and a coach case
   for it (the Not-Halt text from this run).
4. Masking: treat "Herr / Frau + surname" as a name even when it matches no directory entry; the coach must not suggest
   disciplinary steps for a named person.
5. Spend limit: check that Musterwerk has a spend limit in the seed the reading gets; if it has, add the paint line as a
   German `over_limit` case.
6. Assistant: hold "answer in the language the employee writes in" against the page language (5 of 6 English questions
   came back in German); never offer to forward a request.
7. Evals: `run_coach.py` should report any exception per case, not stop on a 429; mark the `injection` read case as
   "filtered by the provider" when the provider refuses it; `problem_only` still fails on `4d44d91`.
8. Azure: raise the `brain-eval` deployment above 50k tokens a minute before anything multi-user.
9. Musterwerk's routing keywords route 45 % of its own cases: add German keywords per row (the D1 note in
   `PILOT_CHECK.md` says so for companies; it holds for the demo company too).
10. Side note, not AI quality: the brain container logs an OpenAI client retry every 15 s with no model call around it,
    since before this run; it looks like the health check calling a model it cannot reach.
